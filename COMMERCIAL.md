You can use, change and share War Room at no cost if you are:

- a non-profit, school, public research organization, or government body,

To ask about a commercial license, open an issue titled **Commercial license** at <https://github.com/tf-anguskong/warroom/issues> (no confidential details needed; we'll take it from there), or contact [@tf-anguskong](https://github.com/tf-anguskong) on GitHub.
